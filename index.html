Compliance Corner

Not legal advice

Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, the CSR Policy Rules, 2014 and Schedule VII — the journey, the rules, the checklist, a compliance simulator and real penalty cases. Confirm against notified text on mca.gov.in.

From voluntary guidelines to penalties — tap a bubble

Dashed bubbles are pending bills. Scroll sideways on mobile.

2009–2013 · Voluntary era

Guidelines and disclosure only. CSR was encouraged, not required.

2014–2020 · Comply or explain

Spending mandated, but unspent money only had to be explained in the Board's report.

2021 onward · Comply or pay

Unspent amounts must be transferred; monetary penalties on the company and officers.

1Does CSR apply, and how much?
Thresholds — any one, in the preceding FY
  • Net worth ≥ ₹500 crore
  • Turnover ≥ ₹1,000 crore
  • Net profit ≥ ₹5 crore

Pending: ₹10 crore profit trigger (2026 Bill) — not yet law.

Obligation

At least 2% of average net profit (computed under Section 198) of the three immediately preceding financial years. New companies use the years available.

Quick calculator
2Governance: committee, policy, action plan
  • CSR Committee of the Board: at least 3 directors with at least one independent director (where the company must have one); a private company may have 2 directors. If the obligation is ≤ ₹50 lakh, the Board can perform the committee's role (Section 135(9)).
  • A company with money in its Unspent CSR Account must have a committee (2022 rules).
  • CSR Policy approved by the Board and disclosed on the website, with committee composition and approved projects.
  • Annual Action Plan: projects, ongoing vs one-time, modalities of fund use, implementation schedule, monitoring and impact-assessment plan.
  • CFO certificate each year that funds were used for approved purposes.
3Schedule VII — eligible activities, with programme ideas
Paraphrased from Schedule VII as amended; item (xiii) per G.S.R. 416(E), 27 May 2026. Programme ideas are illustrative — Schedule VII items should be read liberally but your project must clearly fit one.
4What to include — and what to exclude
Include
  • Projects under any Schedule VII item, delivered in India
  • Delivery directly, via your own Section 8 foundation, or via a CSR-1-registered agency
  • Admin overheads up to 5% of the year's CSR spend (staff, monitoring, capacity building of own staff)
  • Impact assessment cost: up to 5% of spend or ₹50 lakh, whichever is less (where IA is mandatory)
  • Capital assets held by an eligible entity (Section 8 company, registered trust/society, public authority) or beneficiaries/SHGs
  • Up to 10% via Social Stock Exchange ZCZP instruments (from May 2026)
  • Collaborations with other companies, if each reports separately
Exclude
  • Activities in the normal course of business
  • Programmes benefiting only employees and their families
  • Contributions to political parties (directly or indirectly)
  • Sponsorships and events primarily for marketing or brand visibility
  • Activities outside India (except training of Indian sports personnel representing India)
  • Activities required by another law (e.g. environmental clearance conditions, labour-law obligations)
  • Funds routed through an implementing agency without CSR-1 registration
  • Surplus from CSR projects added back to business profit
5Unspent money, ongoing projects and set-off
Ongoing project

A multi-year project (up to 3 years excluding the start year) approved as ongoing by the Board. Unspent → Unspent CSR Account within 30 days of year end; spend within the next 3 years; leftover → Schedule VII fund within 30 days after the 3rd year.

One-time

Unspent → a Schedule VII fund within 6 months of year end (e.g. PM National Relief Fund, PM CARES, Swachh Bharat Kosh, Clean Ganga Fund).

Penalty — Sec 135(7)

Company: lower of 2× the amount due or ₹1 crore. Each officer in default: lower of 1/10 or ₹2 lakh — paid personally.

Excess spend can be set off over the next 3 years, with a Board resolution.

6Implementing agencies, impact assessment, capital assets
  • CSR-1: agencies (Section 8 companies, registered trusts and societies with 12A/80G, or government-established entities) must be registered with MCA; non-company-promoted agencies need a 3-year track record. The form was revised from 14 July 2025.
  • Impact assessment: if the average CSR obligation of the 3 preceding years is ≥ ₹10 crore, independent impact assessment is required for projects of ≥ ₹1 crore completed at least a year earlier; attach to the annual report.
  • Capital assets: must be held by an eligible entity or beneficiaries, not the company.
  • Utilisation: money disbursed to an agency counts, but the Board must satisfy itself it was used as approved — collect utilisation certificates and field evidence.

Assurance standards

  • ICAI SSAE 3000 / 3410 — Indian standards for sustainability and GHG assurance, used for BRSR Core.
  • ISSA 5000 — IAASB's overarching sustainability assurance standard; ISAE 3000 / 3410 remain in use.
  • AA1000AS — stakeholder-focused assurance standard.
  • Limited vs reasonable — limited gives negative assurance ("nothing came to our attention"); reasonable gives a positive opinion and needs stronger controls.
Policies
  • Environment, energy, water, waste
  • Human rights, POSH, health & safety
  • Anti-bribery, whistle-blower, code of conduct
  • Supplier code, data privacy
  • CSR policy (Principle 8)
Records
  • GHG inventory & emission-factor log
  • Energy, water, waste meter data and invoices
  • EHS incident and LTIFR registers
  • HR data: headcount, gender, wages, training
  • Grievance and complaints logs
Reports & sign-offs
  • Materiality assessment
  • BRSR / sustainability report
  • Assurance or assessment statement
  • Value-chain partner assessments
  • Board ESG/risk committee minutes
